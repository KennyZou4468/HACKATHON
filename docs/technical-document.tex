\begin{abstract}
This document describes an OpenClaw-based AI Product Selection Agent for lawful consumer products. The system combines natural-language need understanding with live Shopee Singapore retrieval and deterministic validation. It produces explainable shortlists in plain shopper language while keeping product facts, uncertainty, and hard constraints separate from model-generated interpretation.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} product selection, agent workflow, marketplace retrieval, validation, Shopee Singapore

\section{Purpose and Current Scope}

AI Product Selection Agent is an evidence-grounded purchase-decision assistant. It helps shoppers describe a need in everyday language, identifies hard requirements and preferences, retrieves relevant Shopee Singapore listings, compares up to three candidates, and explains trade-offs in the customer's language.

The current implementation is category-neutral. It supports lawful consumer categories such as laptops, shoes, appliances, and furniture. Shopee Singapore is the current live catalogue source, so the Agent does not imply complete market coverage or availability outside that marketplace.

\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Design goal} & \thead{Implementation} \\
\toprule
Useful with little friction & The Agent creates a session-local Preference Profile and asks at most one normal question before the first shortlist when the answer could materially change the decision. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Evidence before recommendation & Live listing facts come from Shopee and a verification layer determines whether a candidate can be described as a verified match. \\
Clear uncertainty & Unknown stock, unbound variant prices, missing attributes, and unsupported performance targets are labelled rather than guessed. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Consistent experience & The workspace output-language policy controls all visible output. The current deployment answers in English while extracting requirements from multilingual input. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Scope boundaries}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The Agent can select, compare, explain, and summarise lawful consumer products. It does not place orders, process payments, or contact sellers.
  \item Unsafe, illegal, fraudulent, controlled, counterfeit, or harmful procurement requests are declined before any marketplace search.
  \item The hardware benchmark knowledge base is used only for relevant computer-component questions; it is not applied to other product categories.
\end{itemize}

\section{Architecture and Decision Flow}

The design separates language reasoning from marketplace facts. The model interprets a request and explains the result. Retrieval tools, source quotes, and deterministic validation control what may be claimed about a product.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{1.36in}Y Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Layer} & \thead{Responsibility} & \thead{Control} \\
\toprule
OpenClaw gateway & Hosts the workspace, Agent, model provider, and tool routing on AWS. & Operational health and provider errors are handled at the gateway layer. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Prompt and Skills & Define identity, scope, language lock, clarification policy, discovery procedure, and response format. & Skills route product selection, live discovery, technical explanations, and sales handoff. \\
Query planning and coverage audit & The LLM proposes one or two semantic retrieval routes and one recovery route; deterministic audits enforce bounded, distinct queries and decide whether recovery is needed. & Query wording improves candidate recall only. It neither changes user hard conditions nor proves a product fact. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Shopee live retrieval & Finds search cards and reads listing details from the configured marketplace. & Search is preference-led; the Agent does not claim wider or complete market coverage. \\
Category-neutral validator & Checks budget evidence, model or variant identity, observed stock, source conflicts, and user hard conditions. & Only literal source observations are checked; unknown is an acceptable result. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Format normalisation audit & Resolves only registered, unambiguous formatting differences after variant verification. & It can never override a failed budget, stock, model identity, source conflict, or a \texttt{not\_met} result. \\
Local knowledge and web research & Supplies technical context or current evidence when appropriate. & Local relative scores never become an FPS claim; web access failures are not bypassed. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Execution flow}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{1.35in}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Step} & \thead{Outcome} \\
\toprule
1. Understand & Extract product category, intended use, budget, hard requirements, preferences, and missing decision-critical information. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
2. Clarify or assume & Resolve contradictory requirements; otherwise ask one high-impact question only when necessary. Visible assumptions may be overridden immediately. \\
3. Plan discovery & Build a structured LLM query plan: core category, decisive specification or alternative vocabulary, and one recovery query. The plan records which hard conditions are omitted from retrieval but remain mandatory for verification. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
4. Retrieve and audit coverage & Search the one or two initial routes, merge and deduplicate cards, and run a deterministic coverage audit. A third recovery query is permitted only when fewer than two plausible same-category candidates were found. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
5. Verify and rank & Apply the budget gate before details, then verify selected models or variants once with the original user requirements unchanged. \\
6. Explain & Return a maximum of three candidates, the recommendation and trade-offs, sources, and one recovery question only when needed. Translate evidence and uncertainty into plain shopper language. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Evidence and Validation Rules}

The Agent preserves the distinction between what the customer asks for, what a source states, and what remains unknown. It does not invent values, aliases, unit conversions, attributes, links, or evidence quotes.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{1.76in}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Evidence or state} & \thead{How the Agent uses it} \\
\toprule
Customer requirement & Kept in its original value, unit, named system, and comparison operator for every candidate in the shopping turn. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Listing observation & Recorded only when the title, description, category, or seller attribute explicitly supports it. Source wording is retained as evidence. \\
Normalised observation & A post-verification audit may accept only a registered safe representation such as spacing in \texttt{500ml} or an explicit \texttt{24 x 500ml} pack expression. Bare shorthand such as \texttt{24s} remains unknown. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Exact listing price & May be checked as a price observation when it is explicitly shown for the relevant offer. \\
Starting or range price & Useful for discovery, but not treated as the final price of a selected variant without variant evidence. \\
Stock unknown & Used unless the source explicitly provides current stock evidence; an accessible listing or sold count is not stock confirmation. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Performance target unverified & Used when no matched benchmark supports the hardware, game, settings, and rendering conditions requested by the customer. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Interaction rules}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A new conversation starts without a prior budget, shortlist, or Preference Profile. Within a conversation, the latest explicit customer update replaces earlier values.
  \item Contradictory requirements may trigger additional clarification, but the system caps consecutive contradiction rounds to prevent an infinite loop.
  \item For any laptop purchase that omits display conditions, the visible default is a built-in 1080p display with no external monitor. For a game or FPS request that also omits graphics conditions, the additional default is medium preset, native rendering, and no DLSS, FSR, XeSS, or frame generation. Other categories do not inherit computer-specific defaults.
  \item Technical specification questions do not launch Shopee unless the customer also requests live products or prices.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Consumer-facing explanation}

Internal evidence states are translated into ordinary shopper language before an answer is shown. For example, the Agent says ``Shopee buyer rating: 4.92 from 486 reviews'' rather than ``seller-listed rating'', and explains that this is buyer feedback on the listing rather than an independent quality test. Similarly, it says ``the product page lists 65 W charging'' rather than ``seller claim'', and names the exact missing fact when a price, stock state, pack count, benchmark, or configuration cannot be confirmed. Plain language never weakens the underlying evidence boundary.

\section{Skills and Operating Controls}

The active workspace uses focused Skills rather than a single unrestricted prompt. Each Skill defines a limited responsibility and hands off to the next step in the decision workflow.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{2.18in}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Resource} & \thead{Role in the system} \\
\toprule
identity, scope, and plain-language references & Set the category-neutral role, lawful-product scope, safety boundary, English output policy, identity answer, and shopper-facing evidence wording. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
product-selection-agent Skill & Runs selection, comparison, listing explanation, and follow-up behaviour; calls final variant verification exactly once. \\
product-selection-discovery Skill & Creates the structured LLM query plan, audits query shape and retrieval coverage, runs bounded Shopee discovery, and applies the category-neutral budget gate before detail retrieval. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
normalisation, response contract, and sales handoff references & Keep safe formatting checks and customer-facing shortlists or human handoff summaries compact, grounded, and language-consistent. \\
technical QA and benchmark knowledge base & Explain hardware in plain language and separate laptop and desktop context; seek current research only when needed. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Regression coverage}

The deployed deterministic regression suite contains 18 automated tests: four route and safety checks, eight query-planning and coverage checks, and six format-normalisation checks. It verifies that technical questions do not start Shopee retrieval without a product or price request; product discovery follows the marketplace route; sales handoff does not trigger a purchase action; query plans remain bounded and distinct; and safe representations such as \texttt{500ml}/\texttt{500 ml} do not create unsafe unit or sizing conversions. These tests protect workflow and evidence boundaries; they do not replace end-to-end review of natural-language quality on live marketplace data.

\subsection{Safety and failure behaviour}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{2.12in}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Condition} & \thead{Response} \\
\toprule
Unsafe or prohibited purchase request & Refuse before retrieval and do not provide sellers, prices, tactics, or workarounds. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Shopee access or login failure & Stop live retrieval, preserve the customer requirement, and do not fabricate alternatives. \\

\section{Current Limitations and Next Steps}

The design is deliberately conservative: a candidate can still be useful to show, but it is not called a verified match when a hard condition lacks evidence. This protects users from false certainty while exposing the information needed for a decision.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{2.67in}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{Navy}\thead{Current limitation} & \thead{Planned improvement} \\
\toprule
Some Shopee listings do not expose a clearly selectable configuration or SKU price. & Improve evidence coverage for these listings while keeping their budget status pending until a chosen configuration can be verified. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
A game-FPS target cannot be proved from component tier, TGP, refresh rate, or a local relative score. & Create a benchmark evidence registry containing source, system configuration, game settings, rendering conditions, and date. \\
Live web research may face bot detection or access limits. & Use reliable source registries and monitor research failures without bypassing access controls. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
Marketplace ranking can hide standard products behind premium, accessory, or exact-phrase matches. & Use the bounded structured query plan, merged results, and a coverage-triggered recovery query; still label results as a retrieved marketplace sample. \\
New categories may require additional product-specific understanding. & Maintain category-neutral verification while adding category-specific explanation references and regression scenarios. \\
\rowcolor{PaleBlue}
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\noindent\textbf{Operational dependencies.} The live deployment depends on the OpenClaw gateway, a configured model provider, Shopee Singapore retrieval and variant-verification tools, and optional web research for current technical evidence. Credentials, runtime configuration, conversation sessions, and logs are intentionally excluded from the project source repository.

\subsection{Conclusion}

The updated Agent is a general consumer-product decision workflow, not a laptop-only chatbot. Its reliability comes from assigning different jobs to different layers: the model understands and explains, live retrieval supplies marketplace evidence, and deterministic validation enforces hard boundaries before the final recommendation is written.
