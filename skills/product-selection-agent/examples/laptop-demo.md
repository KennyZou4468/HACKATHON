# Example: Laptop Decision

## Customer input

`I want a laptop under SGD 3,000 for CS2 at around 200 FPS, coding, and running many programs.`

## Expected behaviour

1. Extract a hard budget of SGD 3,000 and uses of gaming, coding, and multitasking.
2. State visible defaults: built-in 1080p, no external display, medium preset, native rendering, and no upscaling or frame generation.
3. Ask one compact clarification only if the meaning of the 200-FPS target prevents useful verification, for example whether the target is medium or high settings.
4. Search listings, inspect plausible candidates, and bind every displayed price to a selected model before calling it in budget.
5. Treat any FPS claim as provisional unless a matching independent benchmark covers comparable hardware, resolution, settings, and rendering features.

## Avoid

- Do not say a product-level Shopee starting price is the price of a 32 GB / RTX configuration.
- Do not infer 200 FPS from an RTX model number or panel refresh rate.
- Do not ask again for the SGD 3,000 budget after it has been supplied.
