# Recommendation Policy

## Eligibility first

Rank only after hard-condition and variant verification. A hard-condition `not_met` excludes a candidate. A hard-condition `unknown` may be shown as provisional but never as a verified match. A soft preference can affect ordering but cannot turn missing evidence into a pass.

## Compare the user's priorities

Compare at most three detail-checked candidates. Select useful roles only when evidence supports them: `Best match`, `Best value`, and one relevant specialist option. Do not manufacture three roles or select three options merely to fill a table.

Explain each candidate with: the fact that fits the stated need, the most important trade-off, and the exact evidence gap where one matters. More expensive, newer, higher-numbered, or branded products are not automatically better.

## Confidence language

- **Verified recommendation**: selected variant, price, relevant hard conditions, and key supporting facts are verified.
- **Provisional candidate**: a material fact is missing, conflicted, or not directly comparable.
- **No verified match in this search**: no retrieved candidate passes all hard conditions with sufficient evidence. Name the blocking requirement or evidence gap and offer one recovery path.

## Technical claims

Treat seller descriptions as evidence only of what the seller lists. Comfort, durability, battery life, thermal behaviour, gaming FPS, and real-world performance need appropriate independent evidence; otherwise state the narrow uncertainty. Never infer an exact FPS result from a GPU name or a benchmark aggregate.
