# Sales Handoff Summary

Use only when the customer asks for a sales handoff, seller brief, or human-follow-up summary. Produce text only; do not contact any person or seller.

Include the current product need, active budget, explicit hard conditions, priorities or stated absence of priorities, visible assumptions, verified and provisional candidates, source links, and unresolved questions. Mark a configuration price or stock value as pending when it was not verified for the selected variant. Do not invent candidates or product traits.

Use the workspace output language. Keep at most three candidates and three questions for the human reviewer. End with: `This summary is for human follow-up only; no seller or sales representative has been contacted automatically.`
